## 1. Training-Free Token-Level Steering for LLM Personalized Co-Writing

- Source: arxiv
- arXiv ID: 2608.06069

### Authors

Wenhao Mao, Chengbin Hou, Weixiao Wang, Jialiang Zhu, Min Liu, Yibin Hao, Hairong Lv

### Abstract

While Large Language Models (LLMs) show great promise for personalization, they often lack specialized domain knowledge. Conventional solutions like fine-tuning struggle with high computational costs and rapid data updates, while Retrieval-Augmented Generation fails to provide fine-grained, token-level steering. Furthermore, chat-based interfaces remain dominant, whereas productive co-writing paradigms have not yet been well exploited beyond the coding domain. To this end, we introduce SteerWrite, a training-free framework designed for personalized co-writing. Our method effectively adapts the base model to specialized domains without gradient updates, with specific designs tailored to small datasets. Experiments demonstrate that SteerWrite achieves state-of-the-art performance across diverse datasets, metrics, and models, significantly reducing human editing effort.

### English TL;DR
SteerWrite introduces a training-free framework that leverages inference-time token-level posterior probability correction to enable personalized co-writing with LLMs, achieving state-of-the-art performance across diverse domains while significantly reducing human editing effort.

## 2. Once a Response, Always a Response: Detecting LLM-generated Text via Latent Prompt Restoration

- Source: arxiv
- arXiv ID: 2608.05741
- Relevance: 4.4

### Links

- Abstract: http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05741v1
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.05741v1
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.05741

### Authors

Hongrui Bao, Yubing Ren, Yanan Cao, Jinhan You, Fang Fang, Shi Wang

### Abstract

Large language models (LLMs) can generate fluent and convincing text at scale, creating growing risks for misinformation dissemination, educational misuse, and platform governance. These concerns make robust detection of machine-generated text increasingly necessary. Recent zero-shot detectors mainly exploit probability-based statistical discrepancies, but they do not explicitly account for the training process of LLMs, which leaves a distinct generation mechanism insufficiently modeled and limits detection robustness. To address this issue, we propose EchoPrompt, a training-free detector based on latent prompt restoration. Our key intuition is that machine-generated text is typically produced conditioned on an upstream prompt, and this hidden dependency can be partially reactivated by prepending a unified generic prefix. Specifically, EchoPrompt restores a generic assistant-response context, measures the induced likelihood gain with an instruction-tuned model, calibrates it against the corresponding base model, and aggregates the resulting differences into a score that quantifies latent prompt dependency. Extensive experiments show that EchoPrompt achieves state-of-the-art performance among zero-shot detectors while maintaining strong robustness across challenging evaluation settings.

### 中文一句话结论
提出EchoPrompt，一种基于隐式提示恢复的无训练检测器，通过恢复通用助手-回复上下文并校准基模型与指令微调模型的似然差，在零样本检测中达到最先进性能。

### English TL;DR
EchoPrompt detects LLM-generated text by restoring a latent assistant-response context and measuring the calibrated likelihood gain between an instruction-tuned model and its base model, achieving state-of-the-art zero-shot detection.

### 中文详细总结
现有零样本检测器主要利用概率统计差异，但未充分建模LLM的生成机制。EchoPrompt假设机器生成文本通常由上游提示条件生成，这种隐藏依赖可通过恢复通用助手样式前缀部分激活。方法包括三步：1) 向输入文本前添加统一助手前缀恢复上下文；2) 分别用指令微调模型和基模型计算似然，并取差值以校准语言规律；3) 将差值聚合为检测分数。实验表明，EchoPrompt在多个基准数据集上达到最先进性能，且对对抗攻击、文本长度变化和代理模型选择保持强鲁棒性。

### 方法 / 贡献

- 贡献：1) 发现机器生成文本对恢复助手上下文有更强依赖；2) 设计基于上下文校准似然比的方法；3) 在多个基准上验证其鲁棒高效。

摘要提到在三个公开基准数据集上进行实验，与多种有监督和零样本方法对比，EchoPrompt取得最先进性能。实验还涵盖多种对抗攻击、不同输入长度和代理模型选择，推理延迟低于0.26秒/样本。具体数据集名称未在摘要中给出。

## 3. Decoupling Perception from Description: Computation-Grounded Representation Alignment between Multivariate Time Series and Language

- Source: arxiv
- arXiv ID: 2608.05238
- Relevance: 4.4

### Links

- Abstract: http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05238v1
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.05238v1
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.05238

### Authors

Xinran Feng, Yi Xie, Chao Zhang, Ruikun Li, Wanyun Ling, Ziyue Li, Chenxi Liu

### Abstract

Training multimodal models to align time series with language runs into a self-supervision trap. The usual recipe asks an LLM to read a series and write a description, so label quality is capped by the perceptual skill the model is supposed to learn. The data can never teach more than the labeler already knows. A second gap makes this worse: most datasets use a single variable, but the patterns that matter (cross-channel correlation, lead-lag structure, co-occurring anomalies) appear only with several variables, right where the labeling LLM's limits are most exposed. These two problems create a trilemma: existing methods are reliable, realistic, or scalable, but none achieves all three. We resolve this by decoupling perception from description. Deterministic code computes a set of statistics from real, open-source multivariate series; the LLM verbalizes those precomputed facts. Perception, which LLMs do poorly, is handled by computation, while the LLM handles expression. This produces CGTime, our 4B-parameter computation-grounded time-series-language model. CGTime outperforms far larger general-purpose models on multivariate understanding tasks: it attains the best multivariate fact score on our held-out benchmark (0.283 vs. 0.173 for GPT-4o-mini and 0.203 for GPT-5.4-nano), a gap that survives Holm-corrected paired significance tests against every baseline. It also states verifiable numerical facts in generated captions more accurately and covers a broader range of statistical properties.

### 中文一句话结论
CGTime 通过将感知任务（确定性统计计算）与描述任务（LLM 表达）分离，解决了多变量时间序列与语言对齐中的可靠性、真实性和可扩展性三难问题，并以 4B 参数实现了远超更大通用模型的 multivariate 理解能力。

### English TL;DR
CGTime resolves the reliability-realism-scalability trilemma in multivariate time-series-language alignment by decoupling perceptual tasks (handled via deterministic computation of statistics) from descriptive tasks (handled by an LLM). It achieves a multivariate fact score of 0.283 on a held-out benchmark, outperforming GPT-4o-mini (0.173) and GPT-5.4-nano (0.203), with significant Holm-corrected paired tests. The model uses real, open-source multivariate series and a fixed set of 169 deterministic metrics for supervision and evaluation.

### 中文详细总结
该论文指出，当前多变量时间序列与语言对齐方法存在一个三难问题：现有方法要么可靠（如使用合成数据但缺乏真实感），要么真实（使用真实数据但标注成本高），要么可扩展（自动生成但标注质量受限于LLM的感知能力），无法同时满足三者。作者提出将感知与描述分离：确定性代码从真实多变量时间序列中计算统计量（如PCA降维、交叉相关、马氏距离等），再由LLM将预计算事实描述为文本。这样，感知由计算完成，LLM仅负责表达，从而避免了LLM在直接解读时间序列时的错误。基于此原则，他们训练了4B参数的CGTime模型。在评估中，CGTime在多变量理解任务上显著优于GPT-4o-mini和GPT-5.4-nano，其生成的描述包含更准确的可验证数值，覆盖更广的统计属性。训练采用两阶段：监督微调（SFT）使用基于统计量的文本描述和问答对，强化学习（RL）使用相同的统计量作为可验证奖励。

### 方法 / 贡献
- **设计原则**：将感知与描述分离，感知由确定性计算完成，描述由LLM完成，以同时实现可靠性、真实性和可扩展性。
- **统计量作为双用途训练信号**：同一组确定性统计量既用于监督微调的地面真值，也用于强化学习的可验证奖励，确保两阶段优化目标一致。
- **PCA降维策略**：对多变量序列使用PCA，将描述复杂度从O(K²)降至O(rK)（r<<K），同时保留跨通道结构信息。
- **模型架构**：使用冻结的MOMENT编码器提取时间序列特征，通过投影器与LLM对接，并加入双通道注意力汇聚生成时间令牌和通道摘要令牌。

### 实验或数据
- **数据集**：使用真实开源多变量时间序列，来源包括ETT变压器遥测、Monash预测档案、ERA5气象数据、FRED-MD宏观经济指标以及空气质量/能源系统数据。
- **评估基准**：在保留的测试集上评估多变量事实得分（fact score）。
- **对比基线**：GPT-4o-mini、GPT-5.4-nano等更大通用模型。
- **结果**：CGTime得分0.283，显著优于GPT-4o-mini（0.173）和GPT-5.4-nano（0.203），且通过Holm校正配对显著性检验。
- **其他指标**：生成描述中可验证数值的准确性和统计属性覆盖范围均优于基线。

### 值得关注点
- 解决了时间序列-语言对齐中一个关键的三难问题，且无需人工标注或合成数据。
- 将统计计算与LLM表达分离，既利用了计算的精确性，又保留了LLM的语言生成能力。
- 训练过程使用难度课程（从粗粒度摘要到精确数值），强化学习阶段使用数值奖励。
- 代码、模型权重和基准测试数据将开源，便于复现和后续研究。

### 局限性
- 论文明确排除了因果推断，因为需要超出观测数据的假设。
- 统计量集合固定为169个指标，可能无法覆盖所有时间序列模式（如非线性、长程依赖等）。
- PCA降维可能丢失部分复杂跨通道结构信息。
- 模型仅4B参数，在极端复杂的多变量序列上可能仍有提升空间。

## 4. Beyond Sentiment: Comparing Traditional NLP and LLM-Based Multi-Dimensional Analysis for Political News Evaluation

- Source: arxiv
- arXiv ID: 2608.05155
- Relevance: 4.3

### Links

- Abstract: http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05155v1
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.05155v1
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.05155

### Authors

Maryam Fooladi, Federico Bottino

### Abstract

Traditional sentiment analysis (SA) models, while effective for polarity classification, provide limited insight into the rhetorical, ideological, and framing dimensions of political discourse -- dimensions that are central to research in the social sciences and humanities (SSH). In this paper, we present a comparative study of RoBERTa-based sentiment analysis and an LLM-based multi-dimensional framing analysis platform applied to a corpus of 50 political news articles from 17 international media outlets. The results reveal a critical limitation we term neutral collapse: RoBERTa classifies 70% of articles as neutral, effectively flattening substantively rich political content into an analytically uninformative category. We find that 23% of neutral-classified articles exhibit negative probability scores above 0.30. By contrast, the LLM-based approach captures political bias direction and intensity, sensationalism, emotional appeal, and political framing -- yielding multi-dimensional analytical outputs aligned with SSH epistemologies. We argue that for political media analysis, traditional SA alone is insufficient, and that LLM-based multi-dimensional frameworks offer a more epistemologically adequate computational lens for SSH research needs.

### 中文一句话结论
传统情感分析（RoBERTa）将70%的政治新闻归类为“中性”，导致信息丢失；而基于大语言模型的多维分析能更有效地捕捉政治偏见、耸人听闻和框架等维度，更符合社会科学与人文学科的研究需求。

### English TL;DR
Traditional sentiment analysis (RoBERTa) suffers from "neutral collapse," flattening 70% of political news into an uninformative neutral category, whereas LLM-based multi-dimensional analysis captures bias, sensationalism, emotional appeal, and framing, better aligning with social sciences and humanities research needs.

- 贡献：(1) 识别并定义了“中性坍缩”现象——情感模型倾向于将政治内容归为中性；(2) 通过案例分析展示信息丢失；(3) 提供结构化比较，论证两种工具的互补而非替代关系

## 5. Position: It's Time to Optimize LLMs for Self-Consistency

- Source: arxiv
- arXiv ID: 2608.05188
- Relevance: 4.3

### Links

- Abstract: http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05188v1
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.05188v1
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.05188

### Authors

Itamar Pres, Belinda Z. Li, Laura Ruis, Zifan Carl Guo, Keya Hu, Mehul Damani, Isha Puri, Ekdeep Singh Lubana, Jacob Andreas

### Abstract

Despite ever-increasing sophistication in language model (LM) pre- and post-training pipelines, many important failures persist: models overcondition on user framing ("sycophancy"), exhibit incomplete logical generalization, and produce confident but incorrect responses. We argue that these failures arise from a modeling assumption permeating all aspects of the pipeline: that behavior can be specified and evaluated independently on single-output pairs. Many model failures are difficult, if not impossible, to detect without reasoning about relationships between a model's responses across inputs. In this position paper, we propose self-consistency as a framework for understanding these failures. We first observe that a wide variety of techniques designed to improve specific aspects of LM behavior-targeting properties as diverse as adversarial robustness and factual coherence-can be understood as special cases of a common "consistency optimization" procedure and addressed with a standard set of optimization tools. We next outline a set of new model properties that could be achieved by optimizing for consistency, and conclude with a discussion of what it would mean to develop generally consistent LMs, including the capabilities they would enable and the objections they raise.

### 中文一句话结论
本文提出将“自一致性”（self-consistency）作为统一框架，用于理解并优化大语言模型的多类失败模式（如谄媚、逻辑泛化不足、事实错误），并主张通过跨输入的输出关系约束来替代单样本独立评估与训练。

### English TL;DR
The paper argues that many language model failures stem from evaluating outputs independently and proposes optimizing for self-consistency—enforcing relational constraints across multiple inputs—as a unifying framework to address these failures and enable new capabilities like self-description and self-improvement.

### 中文详细总结
当前大语言模型尽管规模不断提升，仍存在谄媚、逻辑泛化不完整、自信但错误等顽固问题。作者指出，这些问题的根源在于一个贯穿训练与评估的隐含假设：即模型行为可针对单个输入输出对独立定义和评估。许多失败只有通过比较模型在不同输入下的响应关系才能被发现。本文提出“自一致性”作为理解这些失败的新框架：将模型行为的一致性约束（如不变性、等变性、元层面自描述与行为的一致性）作为优化目标，而非仅优化单点损失。作者首先展示大量现有技术（如对抗鲁棒性、事实一致性、自我批评等）可被视为一致性优化的特例；然后提出利用一致性可以定义新的模型能力（如自我描述、自我红队、自我改进）；最后讨论了实现一般一致性模型的意义与潜在反对意见。

### 方法 / 贡献
- **提出统一框架**：用一致性函数 r(x,x') 约束相关输入对的输出关系，并给出三类优化方式（硬约束、软正则、后验正则）。
- **区分两类一致性**：实例级（invariances/equivalences）和元级别（self-description 与 self-alignment）。
- **统一现有方法**：将对抗鲁棒性、事实一致性、忠实链式思考、自我训练等归纳为一致性优化的特例。
- **提出新能力**：通过元级一致性可实现自我红队、假设生成、自我改进等。
- **强调无需额外数据**：一致性约束利用已有数据的内在结构，不依赖外部标注。

### Abstract

### Abstract

Post-training adaptation has become central to modern machine learning practice and includes techniques such as retraining, fine-tuning, parameter-efficient adaptation, alignment, retrieval augmentation, model editing, unlearning, calibration, and Multimodal Instruction Tuning. However, the literature remains fragmented across technique families, model classes, and deployment contexts, making it difficult to compare methods or describe how a trained model has been modified. This survey synthesizes the post-training adaptation literature and introduces a six-dimensional taxonomy organized by mechanism, goal, data requirement, persistence, structural scope, and model type. The taxonomy distinguishes commonly conflated terms such as fine-tuning, retrieval augmentation, and prompting, and shows how adaptation strategies evolve from traditional machine learning through deep learning, foundation models, large language models, and multimodal large language models. It also maps relationships among techniques, including inheritance, supersession, hybridization, and layered deployment stacks. The resulting vocabulary can support technical documentation, model-change tracking, and governance analysis. The survey concludes by identifying open challenges in evaluation, reproducibility, persistent inference-time adaptation, unlearning, multimodal adaptation, and governance-aware post-training workflows.
